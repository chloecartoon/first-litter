Rules
  • Always inside a white sticker circle — pastels live on the surrounding card or page, not behind her.
  • Match expression to context (don't show Excited on a labor-warning card).
  • Idle wiggle on Home only — every ~8s, never on info screens.
  • Sticker sizes: 32pt (chip), 48pt (greeting), 100pt+ (empty states & celebrations).
  • Never recolor Snoopy. Black ears, white fur, pink tongue — always.
