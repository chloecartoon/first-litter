  1. Move {mom} to her whelping box (or wherever she's chosen — don't fight her).
  2. Dim lights. Keep household quiet. Only her favorite people in the room.

  4. Tap Start Delivery Mode below. Supplies within arm's reach.

When each puppy is born — 8 steps

Most of the time {mom} handles this. Your job: watch, log, step in only if something's wrong.

  1. Puppy emerges in sac. {mom} usually licks it open within 1 min. If she doesn't (common with first litters), gently tear the sac away from the face.
  2. Clear the airway. Wipe nose + mouth. If there's fluid, use bulb syringe: squeeze, insert, release.
  3. Rub briskly with towel to stimulate breathing. They should cry/squeak within 30 seconds.
  4. Cord: {mom} usually chews through it. If not — tie thread ~2.5cm from belly, cut on placenta side with sterilized scissors. Dab stump with iodine.
  5. Place puppy at a nipple. Nursing in first hour gives colostrum (immunity).
  6. Log the puppy in the app — time, sex, weight, markings, collar color.
  7. Reset timer. Next puppy usually 30–60 min later (up to 2 hours).
  8. Placenta follows each puppy within 15 min. {mom} may eat 1–2 (normal). Count them — should match puppies.
Healthy newborn signs
  • Pink tongue and gums
  • Active, moving, crawling toward warmth
  • Crying when picked up
  • Latches and nurses within first hour
  • Feels warm (cold puppy = emergency)
⚠ Emergency — weak or distressed puppy
  • Not breathing: rub vigorously with towel; gently swing head-down (supporting head + neck) to drain fluid; repeat.
  • Cold: warm in your hands or against your skin. Never put a cold puppy directly on a heating pad.
  • Weak / not nursing: rub small dab of Karo syrup on gums.
  • Still struggling after 5 min → SOS to vet.
{mom} is done when
  • She stops straining and relaxes
  • She's calmly nursing/cleaning puppies
  • All expected placentas have passed
  • > 2 hours since last puppy with no contractions
Vet check within 24–48 hrs after delivery — confirm everything passed.
